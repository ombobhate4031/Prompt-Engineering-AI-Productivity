05. AI Productivity Toolkit

1. Introduction

The AI Productivity Toolkit is a collection of reusable AI prompts designed to improve productivity and save time in everyday tasks.

It provides practical prompt templates for email writing, resume creation, summarization, coding assistance and research.

2. Email Writing Assistant

Objective

To generate professional and well-structured emails using AI.

Reusable Prompt

"Act as a professional email writer. Write a polite and professional email about the following topic: [Enter Topic]. Keep the email clear, concise and grammatically correct."

Example

Prompt:
"Write an email to my professor requesting permission to attend a workshop."

Expected Output:
A professional email with a greeting, request, explanation and closing.

Benefits

- Saves time.
- Improves communication.
- Reduces grammatical errors.

---

3. Resume Creation Assistant

Objective

To create professional and ATS-friendly resumes.

Reusable Prompt

"Act as a professional resume writer. Create an ATS-friendly fresher resume using my education, technical skills, projects and achievements. Use professional language and do not invent any information."

Benefits

- Creates structured resumes.
- Improves professional presentation.
- Highlights technical skills.

---

4. Text Summarization Assistant

Objective

To convert lengthy information into short and meaningful summaries.

Reusable Prompt

"Summarize the following text into five important bullet points. Use simple English and include only the most relevant information."

Benefits

- Saves reading time.
- Helps in preparing notes.
- Makes information easier to understand.

---

5. Coding Assistance Assistant

Objective

To generate, understand and debug programming code.

Reusable Prompt

"Act as an experienced programming assistant. Analyze the following code, identify errors, explain the problems and provide the corrected code with comments. Also explain the solution in simple English."

Benefits

- Helps beginners learn programming.
- Identifies coding errors.
- Improves code readability.
- Supports problem-solving.

---

6. Research Assistance Assistant

Objective

To help users understand technical topics and perform research.

Reusable Prompt

"Act as a research assistant. Explain the following topic with an introduction, important concepts, real-world applications, advantages, disadvantages and conclusion. Use simple and clear language."

Benefits

- Organizes research information.
- Helps understand complex topics.
- Saves research time.
